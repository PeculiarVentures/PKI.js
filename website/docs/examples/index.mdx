# Examples

Every example is a complete script. Press **Run** to execute it in your browser with the current version of PKIjs, or copy it into your project.

## Certificates and revocation

- [Create and validate a certificate](certificates-and-revocation/create-and-validate-certificate.mdx): a root CA and an end-entity certificate, parsing, and chain validation.
- [Working with certificate requests](certificates-and-revocation/working-with-certificate-requests.mdx): create a PKCS#10 CSR and check it as a CA.
- [Creating and parsing CRLs](certificates-and-revocation/creating-and-parsing-CRLs.mdx): revoke a certificate and check revocation.
- [Working with OCSP requests](certificates-and-revocation/working-with-OCSP-requests.mdx): ask for a certificate's status.
- [Working with OCSP responses](certificates-and-revocation/working-with-OCSP-responses.mdx): answer with a signed status and verify it.

## Signing and encryption with CMS

- [Working with CMS signing](signing-and-encryption-with-CMS/working-with-CMS-signing.mdx): sign data and verify the signature and its certificate chain.
- [Working with CMS certificate-based encryption](signing-and-encryption-with-CMS/working-with-CMS-certificate-based-encryption.mdx): encrypt for RSA and elliptic-curve certificates.
- [Working with CMS password-based encryption](signing-and-encryption-with-CMS/working-with-CMS-password-based-encryption.mdx): encrypt and decrypt with a password.
- [Working with PKCS#7 certificate bags (P7B)](signing-and-encryption-with-CMS/working-with-PKCS-7-certificate-bags-P7B.mdx): bundle and list a certificate chain.

## Timestamping

- [Creating a timestamp request](timestamping/creating-a-timestamp-request.mdx): ask a time-stamping authority for a timestamp.
- [Creating a timestamp response](timestamping/creating-a-timestamp-response.mdx): issue a timestamp and verify it.

## Other

- [Working with PKCS#12 files](other/working-with-PKCS-12-files.mdx): store a key and its certificate in a password-protected file.
